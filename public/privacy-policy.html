Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MotoParts ERP system ("we", "our", "the System") collects, uses, and protects information when you connect your Shopee and/or TikTok Shop seller account(s) to our internal order, inventory, and finance management system.

1. Who we are

This System is an internal tool built for our own e-commerce store to manage orders, inventory, and finances across Shopee and TikTok Shop. It is for self-use only and is not sold, licensed, or made available to other merchants.

2. Information we collect

  • Platform authorization data: When you connect a Shopee or TikTok Shop store via OAuth, we receive and store an access token and refresh token that let us call the platform's Open API on your store's behalf.
  • Order data: Order number, buyer name, phone number, shipping address, product/SKU, quantity, price, shipping fee, order status, and tracking number, as returned by the platform's Order API.
  • Product data: SKU, product name, and stock quantity you maintain in the System.
  • Account data: The email address used to log in to the System.

3. How we use this information

Data is used solely to operate the System's own features: displaying and managing orders, tracking inventory levels across warehouses, computing profit/loss, and keeping order status in sync with the connected platform. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Data storage and security

Data is stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database with Row Level Security enabled, meaning only authenticated users of this System can read the data. Access tokens are stored server-side and are never exposed to the browser. Connections to our database and to Shopee/TikTok Shop APIs use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.

5. Data retention

Order and product data is retained for as long as needed to operate the store. If you disconnect a store, we stop syncing new data for it; you can request deletion of previously stored data by contacting us using the details below.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the email below.
